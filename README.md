# Privacy Policy for Sky Hopper

**Effective date:** [Effective Date]

[Developer/Company Name] ("we", "us") operates the mobile game **Sky Hopper**
(the "App"). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it's
used, and your choices.

## Summary

- Sky Hopper has **no user account, no login, and no in-app purchases**.
- We do not directly collect your name, email, or any personal profile.
- The App shows ads via **Google AdMob**, which collects device/advertising
  identifiers to serve and measure ads, as described below.
- If our Console listing declares a mixed audience, the App asks a neutral
  age question the first time it's opened and adjusts the ads it requests
  accordingly (see "Children's Privacy").

## Information We Collect

**Directly, by us:** none. Sky Hopper stores your progress (coins, unlocked
skins/pets/worlds, high scores, settings, and your age-screen answer) only
**locally on your device**. We do not operate a server that receives this
data, and we cannot see or access it.

**Via Google AdMob (our advertising partner):** to show and measure ads, the
Mobile Ads SDK can collect:
- Advertising ID / device identifiers
- General device information (device model, OS version, language, time zone)
- IP address (used to derive an approximate, non-precise location)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and similar ad-performance events)

AdMob may use this data to show interest-based ("personalized") ads, unless
you are shown and decline the consent form described below, or unless you are
treated as a child (see "Children's Privacy"), in which case only
non-personalized, contextual ads are requested. See Google's own policies for
full detail on what the Mobile Ads SDK collects and how Google uses it:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data when you use partner sites/apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob / Google AdMob data disclosure: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

## Consent (EEA / UK users)

The first time the App is opened in the European Economic Area or the UK, it
shows Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form, which lets you
choose whether to allow personalized advertising. Your choice is stored on
your device and can change what kind of ads (personalized vs. non-personalized)
you're shown afterward.

## Children's Privacy

Sky Hopper is a general-audience game that may also appeal to children. The
first time the App is opened, it asks a neutral question (what year you were
born) so it can determine whether to treat the session as a child's:

- If you indicate you are under 13, the App requests **child-directed,
  non-personalized, general-audience (G-rated) ads only** from AdMob for that
  device, and does not request personalized advertising.
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what
  is described above (device/advertising identifiers used solely to serve
  non-personalized ads).

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information beyond this,
please contact us at pedrosasilva95@gmail.com and we will address it.

## Data Retention & Sharing

We do not sell your information. Data collected by AdMob is handled under
Google's own policies (linked above) and is used for the purposes described
there (serving ads, measurement, fraud prevention). We do not share data with
any other third party.

## Your Choices

- **Ad personalization:** re-open the consent form via your device's Google
  settings (Settings > Google > Ads) to change your personalized-ads choice
  at any time, or reset your device's advertising ID.
- **Uninstalling** the App removes all locally stored progress and settings
  from your device.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. Continued use of the App after
an update means you accept the revised policy. The effective date above
reflects the latest revision.
