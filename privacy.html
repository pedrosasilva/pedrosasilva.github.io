Sky Hopper Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Information we collect

Sky Hopper does not require an account and we do not collect your name, email address or other personal details. Your game progress (coins, purchases, settings) is stored locally on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address and general usage data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android device settings. See Google's advertising policy for details.

Children

Sky Hopper is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with Google AdMob as described above.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page.
